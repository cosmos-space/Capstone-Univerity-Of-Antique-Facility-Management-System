(GSU holds most of the facilities other then the colleges avr and holds non college owned equipments)
1.) College A --(sends form request)--> GSU(recieves)
2.) GSU (check if a. booking other colleges avr/equipments or b. only borrowing the university facilities and equipments, c. if in some way equiments cant be covered by GSU, allow for GSU to book equipments themselves to other colleges) 

--(a)--> GSU checks availability (if available accept, if not attempt reschedule, by some means the GSU can't then finally deny).
--(b)--> GSU Checks availability, if there is data (if available accept, if not attempt reschedule, by some means the GSU can't, then finally deny), if no data --(forwards request to College B)--> College B (can confirm/deny/reschedule availability of their avr or equipment) --(response forwards to GSU)--> GSU (verifies) --(GSU final verdict forward to College A).
--(c)--> GSU checks  if possible but needs to book the rest of the equipment from other colleges --(send request to college B)--> College B denies/accepts on
